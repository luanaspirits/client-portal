Guided Exercises

Go slowly. There's no right way to do these.

Releasing the sputter

For the Three of Wands reversed and the Eight of Cups: letting go of the disappointment, not the person.

  1. Sit somewhere quiet and take three slow breaths.
  2. Picture the engine starting up with all that firepower, and then sputtering out. Notice where you feel it in your body.
  3. Let yourself feel it fully: the disappointment, the injustice, the sour taste. Don't rush it.
  4. On an exhale, imagine setting down that feeling like a cup you've carried too long, and walking away, like the figure on the Eight of Cups.
  5. Say out loud: "I release the disappointment. I keep the lessons. I'm open to receiving love."
The oxygen mask check-in

For the Sun reversed and the Six of Pentacles reversed.

  1. Once a day, ask yourself: "What do I need right now?"
  2. Name one thing, however small: rest, water, a walk, quiet, a hug.
  3. Give it to yourself before you give your next thing away.
  4. Notice any guilt that comes up, and question it: "I can only take care of others when I take care of me first."
Say it when you feel it

For the Two of Swords, Queen of Cups reversed and Seven of Swords.

  1. When you notice yourself biting your tongue, pause and name the need to yourself.
  2. Share it simply and warmly: "Hey, I would really like to do this. It's really important to me."
  3. Invite Justin to do the same: "If there's ever something you need, I want you to tell me."
  4. Afterwards, write down how it felt to be heard.
Surrender breath

For the Four of Pentacles reversed, the anchor of your reading.

  1. Clench your hands into fists, like the man clutching his pentacles. Hold for five seconds.
  2. Slowly open your hands, palms up, as you exhale.
  3. Repeat three times, and on the last exhale say: "I don't have to hold everything so tightly. I'm safe to receive."
The Incredibles reminder

For the Page of Pentacles and your children.

  1. Rewatch the scene from the plane crash to when the kids discover their own powers.
  2. Write down one superpower you see in each of your four children.
  3. Choose one place this week where you can trust them to use it, and step back.
