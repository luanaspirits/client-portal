What we uncovered

Is it my dream or my wound?

Both, and they were never separate. Think of a trampoline: the further down it presses, the higher it launches you. What you've been through, and the urge it gives you to go in the opposite direction, is your purpose.

"Whatever is paining you, and you have the urge to go in the opposite direction, that's your purpose."

So yes, it's okay to dream of the stage. It fires you up, gives you ideas faster and helps you heal faster.

Meet "The Fame Seeker"

The part of you that you were afraid to call vain, attention-seeking or "narcissistic." When we looked at what she actually gives you, she sounds pretty badass:

Expression
Not holding back. Saying what you mean.
Freedom
Not giving a sh*t what people think.
Boundaries
Not taking on other people's baggage.
Charisma
Making people enjoy your presence. Changing the energy of a room. Uplifting.

The protector underneath

There's also a protector part that pushes her down. She's trying to keep you kind and safe: making sure no one feels unheard or shrinks around you, that you don't take up too much space, get so caught up in being right that you dismiss others, or manipulate anyone. She learned this from being told you laugh too loud, talk too much, talk too fast.

She can rest now. You can be the kind of charismatic that lifts other people up, and take up space.

Reframes to keep

"Is something wrong with me?"

Ask instead: "Is there something wrong with me, or is their energy incompatible with mine?" Are they expanded enough to be in my space, or still working through things that make it hard for them to see me shine?

Your soul tribe wants to hear you

The right people make the whole room feel like a stage. Anyone who asks you to shrink for them isn't your soul tribe.

Pass the hot potato

You don't have to be hypervigilant about how everyone feels. With friends you can say: "If you ever feel like I'm talking too much or want to shift the focus, just tell me, no hard feelings." Then it's their responsibility to bring it up, and you can let the heaviness go.

You're allowed to want it

Spiritual people often carry old programming (like a past-life vow of poverty) that says we must be pure, unattached and never want fame, money or visibility. Suppressing those wants doesn't make you more spiritual. It just splits you in two. Want it because you want it.

Don't judge the outside yet

Not judging your client numbers or your views right now. When you're integrating inner conflict it can feel like inching along, and then once the resistance resolves, you shoot forward.

Your practices

When you freeze in front of the camera

Don't try to talk yourself into it. Get curious instead. Bulldozing a scared part pushes her down further.

1
Set up to film. Notice the constriction and name it out loud: "I see that I'm feeling constricted. I see that I'm tense."
2
Ask the part: "How are you feeling? What's scary to you about this? What do you want to say to me?" Keep the back-and-forth going.
3
Don't push for "we're posting no matter what." Listen to understand and offer safety.
4
Imagine the little one holding onto your leg. Pick her up, hold her close, reassure her. Then you, the adult, walk forward.
5
Make the video. Shaky voice, retakes, imperfect: all welcome. Publishing is the accomplishment. Content is a practice.
The soul connection mirror

For the confusion around Arnav. Make two lists. Everything that attracts you is a quality you're meant to embody more. Everything that repels you may be a part of your shadow you've disowned. It's mirror work, parts work and shadow work in one.
