Guided exercises

Tools for this chapter

Open one when you need it. Each one comes straight from something that came through in your reading.

Put the Suitcases Down

For when you catch yourself carrying someone else's weight
From: Nine of Wands reversed, your side of the block
  1. Close your eyes and picture yourself holding suitcases. Notice how many there are and whose they are.
  2. For each one, ask: "Is this mine?"
  3. If it isn't, imagine setting it gently on the ground and saying, "I can care about you without carrying this for you."
  4. Shake out your hands and arms. Notice how much lighter your body feels.
  5. Write down one thing you're releasing responsibility for today.

The 90-Second Feel-Through

For little disappointments and triggers along the way
From: Page of Cups reversed in your final message, and the natural evolution of an emotion
  1. When a trigger hits, name it out loud: "I'm feeling disappointed" (or hurt, sad, anxious).
  2. Set a 90-second timer. Let the feeling move through your body without fixing it or forcing it positive.
  3. Breathe into wherever you feel it most.
  4. When the timer ends, notice what's left. Often it's relief, and then a natural inspiration toward a new way of feeling.

This is the opposite of "fake it till you make it." You clean the tank by actually feeling it.

Hermit's Lantern Walk

A weekly walking meditation for Year 1
From: The Hermit (Year 1), and walking meditation from your Blueprint reading
  1. Walk for 15 to 30 minutes with no goal: not productive, not fixing, not healing anything.
  2. Imagine carrying the Hermit's lantern at your heart, lighting just the next few steps.
  3. Ask one question as you walk: "What is true for me right now?"
  4. Don't chase the answer. Notice what comes up and write it down when you get home.

The Forgiveness Release

Letting go of past life and present life pain
From: Justice, Three of Swords, and Five of Pentacles reversed in your soul contract
  1. Write a letter you will never send. Begin with, "I release the pain of..."
  2. Write everything, including what feels like it belongs to another lifetime.
  3. End with: "I forgive from love. I choose a new beginning from self-love."
  4. Safely burn it, tear it up, or bury it. Forgiveness here is for your freedom, not an invitation back in.

Calling in the King of Cups

Getting clear on the love you're lining up with
From: King of Cups, Ace of Cups, The World, The Lovers
  1. List how you want to feel in a partnership (for example: safe, received, calm, cherished).
  2. List the qualities that came through: patient, emotionally mature, giving, wise, holds space.
  3. Next to each one, write how you'd recognize it in real life. What does patience actually look like on a hard day?
  4. Keep this list. When someone new shows up, let it be your castle gate: vet emotional maturity before you let anyone in.

The Long-Term Decision Check

For whenever you're weighing the connection
From: your final advice and the Year 2 fork

  2. Ask: "Which option would the Laura of Year 3, the King of Swords version of me, choose?"
  3. Ask: "What does this person actually have the emotional capacity to give right now?" Look at actions, not feelings.
  4. Choose what's better for you in the long term, even if it's harder in the short term. Don't rush it. Sit with it first.
